MATRIX OPEN FRAME - REVISION 4

Latest requested changes:
  - Uniform 4 mm thickness, with no raised PCB or lever-connector posts.
  - PCB and lever connector seat directly on the frame at Z=4 mm.
  - Six insert holes run all the way through the frame.
  - Each hole has a shallow conical receiver on its top face.
  - Lever connector translated 14.5 mm left toward the PCB, without rotation.
    Its modeled left long body edge touches the vertical crossmember's right
    edge at X=100.9 mm. Lever screws stay on the horizontal centerline.
  - Corner cutouts reduced to 13 mm radius / 26 mm diameter, centered on the
    factory corner insert positions. Detached corner tips are omitted so the
    cutouts remain open. Magnetic standoffs attach to the panel itself.

INSERTS AND RECEIVERS

Six M2.5 heat-set inserts. Default through bore is 4.0 mm diameter, suitable
for the previously selected 4.6 mm outside-diameter insert family after heat
setting. M2.5 is only the internal thread size; check the outside diameter
against the sizing coupon if using a different insert.

The top conical receiver is 0.35 mm deep, widening from 4.0 to 4.7 mm diameter
(90-degree included cone). This is a lead-in, not a counterbore or screw seat.
No printed floor remains in any of the six holes.

To keep both frame faces flush, use inserts no longer than 4 mm. CNC Kitchen
offers M2.5 x 4 mm inserts. If using the earlier 5.7 mm long inserts, a top-flush
installation protrudes 1.7 mm beneath this frame. Check the real panel cavity
at each location, and do not seat longer inserts against the matrix PCB.
Install inserts before attaching the frame to the matrix.

Nominal screws:
  4 x M3 x 8 mm countersunk screws for panel midpoint inserts. About 4 mm
  projection below the frame; verify real panel thread depth before tightening.
  Printed clearance 3.4 mm; top countersink 6.4 mm, depth 1.5 mm.

  4 x M2.5 x 6 mm screws for the controller. With a 1.6 mm PCB these extend
  4.4 mm below the PCB: about 0.4 mm below the frame underside if no washer.
  A thin washer can reduce that projection; verify against the actual insert.

  2 x M2.5 x 6 mm screws plus thin washers for the lever connector. Assuming
  2.2 mm ears and 0.4 mm washers, thread engagement is 3.4 mm. Actual ear
  thickness/underside seating was not measured. Confirm seating and screw
  length on the fit check; neither component should bend as screws tighten.

ORIENTATION AND CABLES

Match the rear-view drawing: controller left, portrait orientation; USB/buttons
at bottom and HUB75 at top. Lever connector right, four-port end above the
horizontal centerline. Panel power is upper right; HUB75 input is lower left.
PCB position/inset and its four mounting centers are unchanged from V3.

Planar route estimates after moving the lever connector:
  Matrix power:      39.4 mm planned / 50.8 mm supplied
  Controller power:  41.4 mm planned / 100 mm supplied
  HUB75 ribbon:     219.1 mm planned / 254 mm supplied

These are drawn centerline estimates. Plug takeoffs, vertical travel and real
bend radius consume slack. Dry-fit the actual harness. The ribbon runs around
the left of the PCB through open frame areas. The Blender cable collection
is hidden by default and can be enabled as an illustrative route.

Nominal modeled closed lever body reaches Z=17.5 mm, with levers about 18.7 mm.
Actual open-lever sweep, connected IDC plug and ribbon bend may project farther.
Choose magnetic-standoff height after fitting the actual components.

PRINT AND ASSEMBLE

1. Print the insert sizing coupon using final material/settings. Check that
   the insert installs straight, holds securely and leaves the required face
   flush. Set it over a clearance hole so a long insert cannot hit the bench.
2. Print the electronics fit-check skeleton and verify all six hole positions,
   seating, underside connector clearance and cables with the real parts.
3. Print the full frame flat side down, conical receivers upward, at 100% scale
   in millimeters. Suggested start: PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 4-5 walls, 5 top/bottom
   layers, 25-35% infill, supports off.
4. Heat-set inserts before mounting to the matrix. Let the plastic cool, check
   the underside and screw projections, then attach via the four midpoint M3
   screws. Fit the PCB and connector without bending either.
5. Route the harness, then install your magnetic standoffs into the exposed
   corner inserts. Two small bottom-border notches can locate a supply tie.

VALIDATION AND FIT ASSUMPTIONS

All three exported printable STLs reload as watertight solids with consistent
winding. Each CSG solid is one connected body. The main part has exactly 4 mm
Z extent. Checks confirm six through bores, 13 mm corner keepouts, centerline
alignment, and exact modeled lever-body/vertical-crossmember edge alignment.

The official board's underside HUB75 socket footprint does not intersect the
frame. Its nominal bottom now reaches Z=-1.55 mm, through an open area of the
frame, rather than resting on plastic. The modeled panel PCB top is Z=-6.7 mm.
Real panel components and ribbon/plug clearances still need a physical check.

Board mounting pitch: 19.685 x 40.64 mm in this orientation, from Adafruit PCB
source. Nominal board inset is still 23 mm from an inner plastic frame edge
estimated 12.7 mm from the outside edge. Lever pitch is your approximately
19 mm measurement. Body/ear envelope and seating remain approximate reference
dimensions, with 13.5 mm interpreted as height. No physical print, harness,
thermal or magnetic-load test has been performed.

BLENDER, DRAWING AND SOURCE

matrix-rear-frame-v4.blend opens to the V4 assembly. Only the object in
"V4 PRINT - frame only" is printable. Other collections are reference panel,
electronics, fasteners and schematic cables. The board is Adafruit's official
mesh; the lever body and panel are illustrative. STL exports are authoritative
for the printed geometry.

layout-v4-actual-size.svg is an A4 reference drawing with overlays. Print at
100% and verify its 100 mm calibration line. The PNG is for on-screen review.

Editable source is in source/: rev4-parameters.json, build_mount_v4.py,
csg.py and draw_layout_v4.py. Install source/requirements.txt in Python, run
build_mount_v4.py, then draw_layout_v4.py. Outputs go to source/output/rev4/.
Generated OpenSCAD files contain the same geometry with baked dimensions;
change the JSON and regenerate to alter parameters.
